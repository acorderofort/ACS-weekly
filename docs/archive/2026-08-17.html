Pathophysiology, prevention, and management of coronary microvascular obstruction.

European heart journal · 2026 Aug 14 · Review/Clinical consensus statement

Cenko E, Badimon L, Vadalà G, Merkus D, Antoniades C, Collet C, De Maria GL, Djordjevic-Dikic A, et al.

Objective. To summarize current understanding of the pathophysiology, diagnosis, prevention, and management of coronary microvascular obstruction and no-reflow after reperfusion for STEMI.

Key results. No-reflow/microvascular obstruction persists in a large proportion of STEMI patients despite timely PCI and is associated with worse infarct healing, adverse remodelling, heart failure, and mortality; current pharmacologic therapies have unclear benefit and several non-pharmacologic strategies show promise but require larger trials.

Clinical relevance. Highlights no-reflow as a common, prognostically important problem after primary PCI and summarizes potential interventional and medical strategies to prevent or treat it.

Why this paper matters. Improved recognition and targeted strategies for microvascular obstruction could reduce post-infarction heart failure and late adverse events after STEMI; this consensus frames priorities for clinical care and research.

Ventricular free-wall rupture, ventricular pseudoaneurysm, and papillary muscle rupture complicating acute myocardial infarction.

European heart journal · 2026 Aug 14 · Review

Lorusso R, Matteucci M, Ronco D, Massimi G, Price S, Czerny M, Estévez-Loureiro R, Siepe M, et al.

Objective. To review the incidence, presentation, diagnostic evaluation, and contemporary management (surgical and transcatheter) of mechanical complications after acute myocardial infarction, including free-wall rupture, pseudoaneurysm, and papillary muscle rupture.

Key results. Mechanical complications are now rare (<1%) but can present with severe haemodynamic compromise; transcatheter options and temporary mechanical circulatory support have expanded management choices alongside surgery, and multidisciplinary Heart Team decision-making is recommended.

Clinical relevance. Provides a current framework for rapid diagnosis and selection of surgical vs transcatheter or supportive strategies for post-MI mechanical complications, emphasizing the role of temporary circulatory support in stabilization.

Why this paper matters. Even though uncommon, these complications carry high mortality; awareness of evolving less-invasive therapies and organized care pathways can improve outcomes in patients presenting after MI with mechanical failure.

Gut microbiota-derived imidazole propionate predicts cardiometabolic risk in patients with coronary artery disease.

European heart journal · 2026 Aug 14 · Prospective cohort study (multi-cohort observational)

Wenzl FA, Wang P, Kahles F, Beck KR, Giannitsis E, Obeid S, Bruno F, Li XS, et al.

Objective. To evaluate whether circulating gut microbiota-derived imidazole propionate (ImP) predicts major adverse cardiovascular events and mortality in patients with acute and chronic coronary artery disease.

Key results. Higher ImP levels were independently associated with more advanced CAD, diabetes, inflammation, and with increased risk of MACE and mortality across multiple ACS and CCS cohorts after adjustment for established risk factors and other gut-derived markers.

Clinical relevance. ImP is a potential prognostic biomarker in patients with CAD that may improve risk stratification for secondary prevention and identifies a possible therapeutic target linked to the gut microbiome.

Why this paper matters. If validated, measuring ImP could refine individualized risk assessment and motivate trials targeting microbiota-derived metabolites to reduce recurrent events in CAD patients.

Associations of the inflammasome-dependent cytokine IL-18 with risk of coronary heart disease.

Cardiovascular research · 2026 Aug 14 · Multicohort prospective observational analysis

Björkbacka H, Gonçalves I, Engström G, Schiopu A, von Sydow A, Ryaboshapkina M, Varma V, Moss A, et al.

Objective. To assess associations between plasma IL-18 levels and risk of myocardial infarction, cardiovascular death, and atherosclerosis severity across large population and disease cohorts.

Key results. Elevated IL-18 was associated with higher risk of MI and CV death in the Malmö cohort and the UK Biobank and with carotid atherosclerosis (weaker than IL-6) in imaging cohorts; IL-18 added little to clinical risk reclassification in UK Biobank.

Clinical relevance. Supports IL-18 as an inflammasome-related biomarker linked to incident ischemic events and a plausible target for therapies addressing residual inflammatory risk in high-risk patients.

Why this paper matters. Findings justify consideration of inflammasome/IL-18–targeted interventions in future randomized trials for residual inflammatory risk, but current incremental prognostic value for routine risk stratification appears limited.

PCSK9 inhibitor treatment and outcomes in patients with atherosclerotic cardiovascular disease but without prior ischaemic events: an observational study.

European heart journal · 2026 Aug 14 · Observational retrospective cohort study (database analysis)

Bhatt DL, Perrone Filardi P, Khan I, Tuñón J, Marx N, Castro Cabezas M, Andrade K, Koumas A, et al.

Objective. To evaluate associations between initiation of PCSK9 monoclonal antibodies and 5-year ischemic events and mortality in real-world patients with atherosclerotic cardiovascular disease without prior ischemic events using propensity matching.

Key results. In 19,670 matched patients, PCSK9 inhibitor initiators had lower estimated 5-year rates of the composite outcome (17.5% vs 25.4%), corresponding to a relative risk reduction of ~31% and absolute risk reduction of 7.8%; on-treatment LDL-C fell by ~63 mg/dL (~54%).

Clinical relevance. Real-world data indicate substantial reductions in MI, ischemic stroke, and all-cause mortality associated with PCSK9 mAb use in high-risk ASCVD populations, supporting intensive LDL-C lowering strategies.

Why this paper matters. These findings reinforce the potential clinical benefit of PCSK9 inhibitors for preventing recurrent ischemic events in high-risk patients and may inform guideline and payer discussions about broader use.
