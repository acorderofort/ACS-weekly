Myocardial Scar Assessment Using Artificial Intelligence-Powered Contrast-Free MRI: A Prospective Multicenter Study of Virtual Native Enhancement.

Journal of the American College of Cardiology · 2026-07-15 · Prospective multicenter blinded diagnostic accuracy study

Zhang Q, Zhou D, Thompson P, Vavillis G, Anderton T, Ahmed R, Gonzales RA, Zhang H, et al.

Objective. To prospectively validate an AI-based contrast-free 'virtual native enhancement' (VNE) CMR technique for detection and quantification of myocardial scar versus late gadolinium enhancement (LGE) as reference.

Key results. VNE diagnosed myocardial infarction with 94.4% accuracy on confident images and 87.5% overall; scar quantification correlated strongly with LGE (R=0.90) with mean difference 3.2%; VNE agreed with LGE on infarcted territories 90% of the time and was judged adequate to avoid LGE in ~69.7% of patients, with comparable diagnostic accuracy (~93.7%).

Clinical relevance. Provides a validated, non-contrast CMR method to detect and quantify MI scar, which could reduce gadolinium use and increase access to myocardial scar assessment relevant to post-MI risk stratification and management.

Why this paper matters. Accurate scar detection influences secondary prevention decisions (risk stratification, device considerations, revascularization planning); a reliable contrast-free method could broaden evaluation options and lower contrast-related risks, making scar assessment more accessible.
